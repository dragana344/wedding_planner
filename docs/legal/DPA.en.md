# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

The text moved to `lib/legal/dpa.ts` (Macedonian and English, as pairs, so `tests/lib/pure/legal-parity.test.ts` covers it) and is published at **/dpa** (Macedonian) and **/en/dpa** (English). Venues accept it together with the Terms of Service at signup; the version and time are stored in `venues.terms_version` / `venues.terms_accepted_at` (migration 0046).
