# Accessibility audit (TEST-007)

Automated: `e2e/a11y.spec.ts` runs axe (WCAG 2.1 A/AA) on the marketing page, login, signup, couple login, venue dashboard, new event, reservations, settings, couple home, guests, invitation and the public invitation page. It runs with the E2E job in CI and attaches every screen's full result to the Playwright report. Rollout per the task: **warn** now; set `A11Y_STRICT=1` in the CI `e2e` job to **fail on serious/critical** once the findings below are fixed.

Fixing is a separate, approved change (it alters markup and colours).

## Findings (29 Sep 2026)

| # | Impact | Issue | Where | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | critical | Inputs without a label | Couple home: contact email ×2 and phone (`.ev-field` inputs); couple guests: party-size number input; venue reservations: date and party-size inputs; (manual) guest form name/phone/notes use placeholders only; invitation "Порака" textarea; NewEventForm contact inputs | Add `<label htmlFor>` (visible) or `aria-label` in Macedonian |
| 2 | serious | Colour contrast below 4.5:1 | Muted text tokens: `.page-sub`, `.meta-lab`, "Наскоро" nav items, auth form labels and `.auth-link`, `#signup-password-hint`, marketing preview stats/caption and footer | Darken the muted colour token(s) (e.g. `--muted`) to ≥ 4.5:1 on their backgrounds; one change fixes most rows |
| 3 | serious (manual) | Page language | `app/layout.tsx` sets `<html lang="en">` while the UI is Macedonian — screen readers use English pronunciation | `lang="mk"` (and `lang="en"` on the English legal pages) |
| 4 | moderate (manual) | Mixed-language UI | `components/invite/RsvpForm.tsx` (guest-facing) is in English; some auth error/status strings in `app/login/page.tsx` and `SignupForm.tsx` are English | Translate to Macedonian |

## Re-run

```bash
npx playwright test e2e/a11y.spec.ts --reporter=list        # prints [a11y] lines
A11Y_STRICT=1 npx playwright test e2e/a11y.spec.ts           # fail on serious/critical
```
